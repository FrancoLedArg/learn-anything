# Research data needs consent, and records which AI taught

The app exists partly to test the theory of learning (`docs/theory-of-learning.md`) and publish the results as academic papers and theses from ITSE (Santiago del Estero, Argentina). That needs ethics committee approval and has to comply with Argentina's personal data law (Ley 25.326). So we decided:

- Anyone 13 or older can sign up and learn.
- A lesson's data is used for research only if the student gave **research consent**. Students under 18 also need a guardian's consent.
- Students can withdraw consent at any time.
- Research data is exported with names and identifying details removed.
- Every lesson records which AI model taught it, because students bring their own AI and different models teach differently.

**Consequences**: data collected without consent can never be used for research afterwards, so consent has to exist from the very first version, not be added later.
